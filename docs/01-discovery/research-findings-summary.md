This document summarizes findings from customer and artisan interviews conducted during Phase 01 — Product Discovery & Problem Definition.

The objective is to identify repeated patterns across participants and determine which Craft BD problem hypotheses are:

- Validated
- Partially validated
- Not validated
- Inconclusive
- Newly discovered

Individual participant opinions should not automatically become product requirements.

# 19. Evidence-to-Requirement Rule

Craft BD will use the following rule:

Repeated User Problem  
→ Meaningful Impact  
→ Supported by Multiple Participants  
→ Feasible Software Response  
→ Requirement Candidate

Feature ideas without sufficient evidence should remain outside the MVP unless they are technically necessary supporting capabilities.

---

# 20. Research Limitations

The following limitations must be acknowledged:

- Small academic research sample
- Participants may not represent the entire Bangladesh handicraft market
- Some participants may have limited custom-order experience
- Self-reported behavior may differ from actual behavior
- Findings should guide the academic MVP, not be treated as nationwide statistical conclusions
