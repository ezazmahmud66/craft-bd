# 1. Product Name

**Craft BD**

---

# 2. Product Category

Craft BD is currently positioned as a:

**Craft Discovery, Artisan Connection, Cultural Information, and Structured Customization Platform**

Commerce functionality supports the overall experience but is not intended to be the project's only value proposition.

---

# 3. Background

Bangladesh has a significant handicraft sector involving individual artisans, craft businesses, traditional production communities, physical retailers, social-media sellers, and established digital platforms.

Customers can already purchase handicrafts through:

- Physical stores
- Craft fairs
- Facebook and Instagram sellers
- Online marketplaces
- Established craft retailers

Therefore, Craft BD should not be positioned simply as another handicraft e-commerce website.

The current product opportunity is to investigate whether digital commerce can provide stronger value by keeping the craft product connected with:

- The artisan
- Cultural context
- Product information
- Production journey
- Structured customization

---

# 4. Provisional Problem Statement

Bangladeshi handicrafts are already available through several physical and digital sales channels.

However, existing product-discovery and social-commerce experiences may not consistently provide a structured way to connect a handicraft product with its artisan, cultural context, production information, and customization process.

Custom craft orders may also involve informal communication through messages, where requirements, quotations, approvals, and progress updates are handled manually.

Craft BD will investigate whether a structured digital experience can improve these interactions for customers and artisans.

---

# 5. Problem Boundaries

Craft BD is **not** intended to solve every challenge faced by the Bangladesh handicraft industry.

## Problems Within Potential Scope

- Digital craft discovery
- Artisan representation
- Structured product information
- Cultural craft information
- Custom-order communication
- Order and quotation tracking
- Product-record access
- Customer-artisan interaction

## Problems Outside Scope

- Artisan financing
- Raw-material shortages
- National export policy
- Transportation infrastructure
- Skilled-worker shortages
- Manufacturing infrastructure
- Nationwide certification regulation

# 6. Primary Target Users

## Customer

Initial target segments:

### Custom Craft Buyer

A customer who wants a personalized handmade product based on requirements such as:

- Design
- Color
- Size
- Material
- Quantity
- Reference image

### Cultural Craft Explorer

A customer interested in learning more about:

- Artisan
- Craft origin
- Materials
- Techniques
- Cultural meaning

---

## Artisan / Craft Seller

Initial target segments:

### Individual Artisan

An artisan who personally creates handmade products and needs a digital way to present products and receive customer requests.

### Social-Media-Based Craft Seller

A seller currently using platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, or similar communication channels.

---

# 7. Provisional Product Vision

> Craft BD aims to create a structured digital experience that connects Bangladeshi craft products with the artisans who create them, the cultural and production context behind them, and the customers who discover, customize, and purchase them.

The platform should help customers understand more than only product price and appearance while giving artisans a structured way to present their craft and manage relevant customer interactions.

---

# 8. Provisional Value Proposition

## For Customers

Craft BD may provide:

- Easier discovery of Bangladeshi handicrafts
- Clear artisan information
- Craft origin and cultural context
- Materials and production information
- Structured custom-order submission
- Quotation approval
- Custom-order progress visibility
- Product and certificate records

---

## For Artisans

Craft BD may provide:

- Structured digital artisan profile
- Product presentation
- Space to explain craft expertise and story
- Organized custom-order requests
- Clear customer specifications
- Quotation management
- Order-status management
- Improved digital visibility

---

# 9. Current Core Product Hypothesis

The strongest current hypothesis is:

> Craft BD may provide more value when it focuses on structured custom craft interaction and craft context rather than competing primarily as a conventional marketplace.

Possible product experience:

Customer  
→ Discovers Craft  
→ Views Artisan  
→ Learns Craft Context  
→ Reviews Product Information  
→ Requests Customization  
→ Receives Quotation  
→ Approves Order  
→ Tracks Progress  
→ Receives Product

---

# 10. Potential Product Pillars

Craft BD currently has four provisional product pillars.

## Pillar 1 — Craft Discovery

Support customers in discovering handmade products by:

- Category
- Artisan
- Craft type
- Region
- Material

---

## Pillar 2 — Artisan & Cultural Context

Connect products with:

- Artisan identity
- Experience
- Expertise
- Origin
- Materials
- Techniques
- Traditional use
- Cultural meaning

---

## Pillar 3 — Structured Customization

Provide a structured workflow:

Customer Request  
→ Artisan Review  
→ Quotation  
→ Customer Approval  
→ Production  
→ Quality Check  
→ Delivery

---

## Pillar 4 — Product Information & Traceability

Provide structured platform-maintained records such as:

- Product ID
- Artisan
- Region
- Materials
- Craft type
- Production information
- Certificate record
- QR-linked record

QR-based access must not be presented as guaranteed proof of physical authenticity without an appropriate verification mechanism.

---
