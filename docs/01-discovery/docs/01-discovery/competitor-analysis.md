# Craft BD — Competitor & Existing Solution Analysis

## 1. Purpose

This document analyzes existing alternatives available to customers and artisans in the Bangladeshi handicraft market.

The objective is to identify:

- What solutions already exist
- Which Craft BD ideas overlap with existing solutions
- Which proposed capabilities may provide additional value
- Which differentiation claims still require user validation

Craft BD should not claim uniqueness without evidence.

---

## 2. Types of Existing Alternatives

Craft BD does not compete only with dedicated handicraft websites.

Users may currently use several alternatives:

1. Established craft retailers
2. Curated handmade marketplaces
3. General online marketplaces
4. Facebook and Instagram seller pages
5. Direct communication through Messenger, WhatsApp, or phone
6. Physical craft stores and fairs

Therefore, competitor analysis should include both direct and indirect alternatives.

---

# 3. Aarong

## Type

Established craft and lifestyle retail organization.

## Verified Strengths

Aarong:

- Has operated since 1978
- Supports more than 87,000 artisans
- Has physical retail stores
- Provides online shopping
- Provides customer accounts
- Provides order tracking
- Offers a large range of fashion, lifestyle, and craft products
- Has strong brand recognition

## Relevance to Craft BD

Aarong demonstrates that:

- Bangladeshi handicrafts already have strong retail channels
- Customers can already purchase craft-related products online
- Supporting artisans through commercial channels is not a new concept

Therefore, Craft BD must not use:

> "There is no digital platform for Bangladeshi handicrafts"

as its problem statement.

---

# 4. JADUZ

## Type

Curated online marketplace for independent Bangladeshi makers.

## Verified Strengths

JADUZ currently provides:

- Maker vetting
- Product approval before publishing
- Maker identity and story
- Maker-specific shop pages
- Handmade product discovery
- Online ordering
- Cash on delivery
- Delivery coordination
- Maker payment management
- Product information connected with the maker
- Bangla and English content

## Relevance to Craft BD

JADUZ creates important overlap with the original Craft BD idea.

The following alone cannot be considered unique:

- Online handicraft marketplace
- Artisan profile
- Artisan story
- Product listing
- Handmade product discovery
- Online ordering

Craft BD therefore needs a more specific value proposition.

---

# 5. Social Media Sellers

## Type

Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, and similar social-commerce channels.

## Potential Strengths

These channels may provide:

- Easy communication
- Direct interaction with sellers
- Low setup cost
- Existing social audience
- Flexible custom-order discussions
- Fast sharing of product photos

## Potential Limitations

The following are currently hypotheses and require user validation:

- Product information may be inconsistent
- Custom-order requirements may be exchanged through unstructured messages
- Customers may need repeated conversations about size, material, design, or price
- Order status may be difficult to track systematically
- Artisan information may not follow a consistent format
- Historical and cultural craft information may not be available

These should not be treated as confirmed problems until users are interviewed.

---

# 6. General Online Marketplaces

## Type

Large marketplaces selling products from many categories.

## Strengths

General marketplaces can provide:

- Product search
- Categories
- Pricing
- Cart and checkout
- Delivery infrastructure
- Ratings and reviews
- Large customer reach

## Possible Gap Relevant to Craft BD

A general marketplace is usually product-oriented rather than craft-context-oriented.

Craft BD may investigate whether users value additional information such as:

- Artisan identity
- Craft origin
- Cultural context
- Materials
- Making techniques
- Production journey
- Structured customization

This remains a product hypothesis until validated.

# 8. Overlap Analysis

The original Craft BD concept contains several features that already exist elsewhere.

## High Overlap

- Product marketplace
- Search and filtering
- Shopping cart
- Checkout
- Artisan profiles
- Product images
- Order tracking
- Reviews
- Maker stories

These features are necessary supporting capabilities but should not be presented as Craft BD's main innovation.

# 9. Potential Differentiation Areas

The following combination may provide a more focused direction:

Craft Product
+
Artisan
+
Cultural Context
+
Production Journey
+
Product Record
+
Structured Customization

### Potential Differentiator 1 — Craft Context

Instead of presenting only commercial product information, Craft BD may connect products with:

- Origin
- History
- Materials
- Techniques
- Traditional uses
- Cultural meaning

### Potential Differentiator 2 — Production Journey

A product may present stages such as:

Raw Material  
→ Design  
→ Making  
→ Finishing  
→ Quality Check  
→ Final Product

### Potential Differentiator 3 — Structured Custom Order

Instead of relying only on chat messages:

Customer Request  
→ Artisan Review  
→ Quotation  
→ Customer Approval  
→ Production  
→ Quality Check  
→ Delivery

### Potential Differentiator 4 — Product Record

A QR code may link a customer to a platform-maintained product record containing relevant product, artisan, material, region, and certificate information.

A QR code must not be described as absolute proof that a physical product is authentic unless a reliable physical verification mechanism exists.

---

# 10. Important Competitive Finding

Craft BD should not position itself as:

> Another Bangladeshi handicraft e-commerce website.

A more defensible direction to investigate is:

> A craft-centered digital platform that keeps the product, maker, cultural context, production information, and structured customization process connected within one experience.

However, this positioning remains a hypothesis until customer and artisan research confirms that these capabilities solve meaningful problems.

---

# 11. Competitive Risks

## Risk 1 — Feature Imitation

Competitors can add similar informational features.

### Response

Craft BD should focus on workflow integration and usability rather than claiming individual features are unique.

---

## Risk 2 — Existing Strong Brands

Large existing organizations already have customer trust and market reach.

### Response

Craft BD should not compete primarily on marketplace size.

---

## Risk 3 — Too Many Features

Attempting to compete with mature e-commerce platforms feature-by-feature would make the student project too large.

### Response

The MVP should focus on the smallest set of features required to demonstrate the validated problem solution.

---

## Risk 4 — Unvalidated Differentiation

Customers may not value production journeys, QR records, or cultural information enough to influence usage.

### Response

These assumptions must be tested through primary user research before final scope decisions.

---

# 12. Current Competitive Position Hypothesis

Craft BD is currently best considered as a:

**Craft Discovery + Cultural Context + Artisan Interaction + Structured Customization Platform**

with commerce as a supporting capability.

This positioning is provisional and will be revised after user research.
