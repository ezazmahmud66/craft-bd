# 2. Current Process

The participant usually discovers handmade products through Facebook pages, recommendations from friends, and local craft fairs.

Before buying, the participant normally checks:

- Product images
- Price
- Materials
- Seller reviews
- Delivery information
- Previous customer feedback

The participant often contacts sellers through Messenger before placing an order.

# 4. Current Workarounds

- Messenger chat
- Screenshots of preferred designs
- Saved Facebook posts
- Manual message searching

# 8. Trust Findings

## What Creates Trust?

- Customer reviews
- Clear seller identity
- Detailed product images
- Materials information
- Previous successful orders
- Clear delivery policy

## What Creates Uncertainty?

- Very limited product information
- No previous customer feedback
- Unclear seller identity
- Price changing during discussion
- Different information given at different times

## Information That May Increase Confidence

- Artisan information
- Materials
- Product origin
- Clear product photos
- Customer reviews
- Consistent product record

# 11. Potential Requirement Candidates

### RC-01 — Structured Custom Request

**Related Problem:**  
Custom-order requirements are fragmented across messages.

**Potential Requirement:**  
The system should allow customers to submit design, size, color, material, quantity, reference image, and instructions in a structured custom-order request.

**Evidence Strength:**  
Synthetic example only

---

### RC-02 — Custom Order Quotation

**Related Problem:**  
Price and design approval currently occur informally through chat.

**Potential Requirement:**  
The system should allow an artisan to submit a quotation that the customer can explicitly approve or reject.

**Evidence Strength:**  
Synthetic example only

---

### RC-03 — Custom Order Status

**Related Problem:**  
Customers manually request progress updates.

**Potential Requirement:**  
The system should display the current status of a custom order.

**Evidence Strength:**  
Synthetic example only

---

### RC-04 — Artisan Information

**Related Problem:**  
Customers may want more confidence and context about who created a product.

**Potential Requirement:**

**Evidence Strength:**  
Synthetic example only

# 12. Researcher Interpretation

The strongest simulated pain point is not ordinary product purchasing.

The strongest potential problem is:

**Unstructured custom-order communication.**

A possible improved workflow is:

Customer Request  
→ Structured Requirements  
→ Artisan Review  
→ Quotation  
→ Customer Approval  
→ Production  
→ Status Updates  
→ Delivery

Cultural information and artisan identity appear valuable but may be supporting capabilities rather than the primary problem.

QR verification is not supported strongly by this simulated interview and should not be prioritized based on this example.

---

# 13. Interview Summary

### Most Important Problem

Custom-order requirements and approvals can become fragmented across chat messages.

### Current Workaround

Messenger conversations, screenshots, and repeated confirmation.

### Main User Need

A clearer way to record requirements, confirm quotation/design, and track progress.

### Possible Craft BD Opportunity

A structured custom-order workflow connected with product and artisan information.
