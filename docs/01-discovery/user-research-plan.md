# Craft BD — Primary User Research Plan

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this research is to validate the current Craft BD problem hypotheses through interviews with potential customers and artisans.

This research should help determine:

- Which user problems actually exist
- Which problems are important
- How users currently solve them
- Which proposed Craft BD capabilities may provide meaningful value
- Which proposed features should be reduced, postponed, or removed

The research should focus on understanding user behavior and problems rather than asking users whether they like specific features.

---

# 2. Research Principle

The preferred interview flow is:

Past Behavior  
→ Current Process  
→ Problems  
→ Impact  
→ Existing Workaround  
→ Desired Improvement

Avoid:

> “Would you like a QR verification feature?”

Prefer:

> “What information helps you trust a handmade product before purchasing it?”

This reduces confirmation bias.

---

# 3. Recommended Participants

For an academic MVP, the initial target is:

## Customers

Target:

**5–8 participants**

Preferred mix:

- People who have purchased handmade or handicraft products
- People who have purchased through Facebook or Instagram sellers
- People who have requested customized products
- People interested in traditional Bangladeshi crafts

## Artisans / Craft Sellers

Target:

**3–5 participants**

Preferred mix:

- Individual artisans
- Small craft sellers
- Facebook/Instagram-based craft sellers
- Sellers who accept custom orders

Total target:

**8–13 participants**

This is sufficient for exploratory academic product discovery, but it should not be treated as statistically representative of the entire Bangladeshi handicraft market.

# 16. Questions to Avoid

Avoid leading questions such as:

- Would you use Craft BD?
- Do you think our idea is good?
- Would QR verification be useful?
- Would an AI recommendation system help you?
- Would you like an artisan profile?
- Would you like to see a production journey?

These questions encourage positive answers and provide weak evidence.

---

# 17. Recommended Interview Method

Preferred format:

**Semi-structured interview**

Duration:

**10–20 minutes per participant**

Possible methods:

- Face-to-face
- Phone call
- Google Meet
- Messenger/WhatsApp call

Interviewers should take notes immediately.

Recording should only be used with participant permission.

---

# 18. Research Ethics

Participants should be informed that:

- This is an academic software engineering project
- Participation is voluntary
- They may skip any question
- Their answers will be used for product research
- Sensitive personal information is not required
- Personal identity should not be published without permission

# 20. Evidence Rule

A single participant statement should not automatically become a product requirement.

Look for:

Repeated Pattern  
+ Meaningful Impact  
+ Relevant Target User  
+ Feasible Software Response

Then consider converting the problem into a requirement.

---

# 21. Research Output

After interviews, findings will be categorized into:

- Validated problems
- Partially validated problems
- Rejected assumptions
- New problems discovered
- Current workarounds
- Potential requirements

These findings will be used to update the Problem Validation Matrix.
