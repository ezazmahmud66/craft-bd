# Craft BD — Initial Market Research

## 1. Purpose

This document records the initial secondary research conducted to understand the Bangladeshi handicraft sector and identify real problems that may be relevant to Craft BD.

The purpose of this research is not to justify previously selected features. Instead, it is intended to identify evidence-supported problems before finalizing the product problem statement and requirements.

---

## 2. Sector Context

Bangladesh has a significant handicraft sector involving traditional crafts, rural artisans, small producers, craft businesses, and larger retail organizations.

Recent sector studies indicate that the handicraft industry has economic potential but also faces several structural and market-related challenges.

PKSF reported that the domestic handicraft market in Bangladesh is estimated at approximately BDT 10,000–15,000 crore annually.

However, Bangladesh continues to face challenges in strengthening the competitiveness and market presence of the sector.

---

## 3. Identified Industry Challenges

The Bangladesh Foreign Trade Institute (BFTI) identified several challenges affecting the handicraft sector through Key Informant Interviews and Focus Group Discussions.

Important challenges include:

- Lack of promotion of Bangladeshi handicraft products
- Weak country branding
- Inadequate linkage between local and global markets
- Limited access to finance
- Shortage of skilled artisans
- Raw-material challenges
- Infrastructure and transportation limitations
- Limited research and design capability
- Competition from lower-cost substitute products
- Difficulties faced by smaller handicraft manufacturers and exporters

Not all of these problems can be solved through software.

Craft BD should therefore focus only on problems where a digital platform can realistically contribute.

## 5. Existing Digital Alternatives

Craft BD will not operate in an empty market.

### Aarong

Aarong is an established Bangladeshi lifestyle and craft organization that works with more than 87,000 artisans across Bangladesh.

It already provides both physical and digital shopping channels and represents a large range of traditional and contemporary craft products.

Therefore, Craft BD cannot claim that Bangladeshi handicrafts currently have no digital sales channel.

### JADUZ

JADUZ is a curated online marketplace for independent Bangladeshi makers.

Its platform already includes:

- Maker vetting
- Maker identity and story
- Handmade product listings
- Online ordering
- Delivery coordination
- Cash on delivery
- Maker-focused discovery

This means that features such as an artisan profile or online marketplace alone are not sufficient to differentiate Craft BD.

## 6. Initial Opportunity

The initial opportunity for Craft BD may lie in combining several types of information and interaction around a craft product.

Possible areas include:

Customer
→ discovers craft
→ learns about artisan
→ understands cultural context
→ views production journey
→ accesses product record
→ requests customization
→ interacts through a structured order workflow

This opportunity is currently a hypothesis and must be validated with potential users.

---

## 7. Problems Craft BD Should Not Claim Yet

At this stage, the project should not claim that:

- Customers cannot buy Bangladeshi handicrafts online.
- Bangladeshi artisans have no online platforms.
- QR codes guarantee that a physical product is authentic.
- Customers definitely require detailed production journeys.
- Customers definitely want QR verification.
- Artisans definitely need a custom-order management system.
- Personalized recommendations are necessary.
- Craft BD will solve the overall economic challenges of the handicraft industry.

These claims require additional evidence or user validation.

---

## 8. Current Problem Hypothesis

The current working hypothesis is:

> Although Bangladeshi handicrafts are available through existing physical and digital channels, conventional product discovery may not consistently keep the artisan, cultural context, production information, and structured customization process connected with the product experience.

Craft BD will investigate whether customers and artisans experience this as a meaningful problem.

This statement is not yet the final problem statement.

---

## 9. Research Questions for the Next Discovery Steps

The next stage should investigate:

1. What information do customers want before purchasing a handmade craft?
2. Does knowing the artisan influence customer trust or purchase decisions?
3. Do customers value information about craft origin and production methods?
4. What problems occur when customers request customized handmade products?
5. How do artisans currently receive and manage custom orders?
6. What makes customers trust information about handmade products?
7. Which existing platforms do customers and artisans currently use?
8. What limitations do they experience with those alternatives?

---

## 10. Initial Research Conclusion

The research does not support positioning Craft BD as simply another handicraft e-commerce platform.

A more defensible product direction is to investigate whether Craft BD can improve the connection between:
